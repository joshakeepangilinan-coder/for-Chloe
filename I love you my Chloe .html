My Love

❦ ❦ ❦

I love you more than anything,

I can't live without you, my love,

Your beauty is a dream come true,

Your voice, a sweet melody.

Your singing is as perfect

As perfect as you are,

And when I see your photo

You're my brightest star.

Your name is a short poem,

So beautiful, so sweet,

My heart keeps beating "Chloe"

With every single beat.

I don't want anyone else, my love,

I only want to love you,

Today, tomorrow, forever —

Only you, my love, only you.

For Chloe ❤️
